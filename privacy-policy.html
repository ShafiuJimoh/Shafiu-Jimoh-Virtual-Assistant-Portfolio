1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used and protected when you visit this website, operated by Shafiu Jimoh ("I", "me", "my"). This site is a personal professional portfolio. It does not sell personal data, run advertising networks, or share visitor information with third parties for marketing purposes.

2. Information Collected

This site collects information in two limited ways:

  • Contact form submissions. When you submit the contact form, the name, email address, subject and message you provide are transmitted to my email inbox through a third-party form delivery service (FormSubmit) so that I can respond to your enquiry.
  • Basic technical logs. The hosting provider for this site (GitHub Pages) may automatically log standard technical information such as IP address, browser type and pages requested, consistent with GitHub's own privacy practices. I do not have access to, and do not separately collect, this data.

This site does not use advertising cookies, does not run third-party analytics trackers, and does not build advertising profiles of visitors.

3. How Information Is Used

Information submitted through the contact form is used solely to respond to your message and to discuss potential work. It is not added to a marketing list, sold, or shared with third parties beyond the form delivery service required to deliver the message to my inbox.

4. Data Retention

Messages received through the contact form are retained in my email inbox for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to and, where relevant, document a business relationship. You may request deletion of your message at any time using the contact details below.

5. Third-Party Services

This site relies on the following third-party services, each governed by its own privacy policy:

  • GitHub Pages, for website hosting.
  • FormSubmit, for delivering contact form submissions by email.
  • Google Fonts and Font Awesome (via cdnjs), for typography and iconography, which may load resources from their respective content delivery networks.

6. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct or request deletion of personal data you have submitted to me. To exercise any of these rights, contact me using the details below and I will respond within a reasonable timeframe.

7. Children's Privacy

This site is intended for a professional business audience and is not directed at children. I do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes in practice or legal requirements. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.
